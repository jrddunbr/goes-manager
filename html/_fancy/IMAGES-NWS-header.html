NWS LRIT Forecast Charts

Human-generated forecast products delivered via the LRIT broadcast. Filenames follow YYYYMMDDhhmmss-product.lrit.gif.

  • atl* — Atlantic surface prognoses (48–72 h) and related overlays.
  • pac* — Pacific surface/precip outlooks.
  • USA_latest — National synoptic charts.
  • hiwind_*, CAR, GULF, EPAC — Specialty aviation/marine graphics.

Charts arrive on a 3–6 hour cycle via the LRIT broadcast and mirror what was received from the satellite downlink.
