EMWIN Bulletins

Text and graphic bulletins distributed via the Emergency Managers Weather Information Network.

  • Text filenames encode WMO header, station, timestamp, and AWIPS ID (e.g., A_SACN85CWAO..._SAHOURLY.txt).
  • High-volume families: surface hourly digests (SAH), weather roundups (RWR), marine summaries (MIS), terminal forecasts (TAF).
  • Graphics (Z_*) include radar mosaics and satellite quicklooks intended for low-bandwidth distribution.
