## Management Guidance
- **Index alignment**: Join the Level-2 catalog with the imagery catalog using `satellite/domain/timestamp`. This enables seamless correlation between raw bands and derived products.
- **Core vs. optional products**:
  - Core severe-weather set: `ACHT`, `ACHA`, `DSI`, `TPW`, `RRQPE`.
  - Optional environmental set: `SST`, `LST` (retained less frequently; ensure targeted archiving).
- **Storage format**: Files are quicklook PNGs. If downstream systems need science-ready grids, consider reprocessing from the original GOES netCDF source or storing georeferencing info extracted from `product.cbor`.
- **Metadata parsing**: `product.cbor` contains ABI Level-2 attributes (scale/offset, units, central lat/lon). Parse once per scan and store in structured form to aid reprojection and quantitative analysis.
- **Quality control**: Watch for scans missing either `Gxx_<code>.png` or its paired `abi_rgb_*`. Gaps may indicate partial reception; use the catalog to flag and re-request downloads if necessary.
- **Retention policy**: Because Level-2 pngs are derivatives, you may opt to regenerate them from primary Level-2 netCDFs when needed. Retain only critical time windows online, keeping the catalog to locate or recreate products.
